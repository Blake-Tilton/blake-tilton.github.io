Story

I started in church media, running whatever needed running on Sunday morning. Then I taught — art first, then graphics and video — and kept ending up with student crews doing work nobody expected from them.

Somewhere along the way, building the crew became the job.

Now I design how technology works for a school district, and I still run media for a church. Same work, different stakes. Set it up right, train the people, and it keeps going after I leave the room.

The Student Tech Program

Forney · Royse City · Community · Mesquite · 2013–2026

Give students real work, a clear process, and someone who takes them seriously. They’ll surprise you. I’ve built that crew four times.

Forney ISD — 6th Grade 2013–2020

Asked to pilot a student media team. Won a grant to fund it. COVID ended it early.

Royse City ISD — Summers Middle School 2020–2023

A new campus, so the A/V program started from nothing. Within two years, 7th and 8th graders covered every media need on campus — and were out-producing the high school.

Community ISD — High School + District Communications 2023–2025

They hired me away over my objections. Split role: teacher, district communications, some IT. The interns became the district’s video crew — sports, promos, a series with the superintendent. 102,000 views in two years.

Mesquite ISD — Student Tech 2025–2026

Came on for instructional tech. Got asked to pilot a student team that repairs student devices. Before the first year was out, the district created a position to take it district-wide.

The part that matters

Several of those students walked into industry jobs straight out of high school. A few of them work for me.

How it works

A facilitator who speaks both tech and instruction. A small group of students chosen for aptitude and attitude. An IT department that trusts them with real tickets. The result: devices back in class faster, and students leaving with work experience instead of a worksheet.

Paradise Baptist Church

Media & Technology Director · 2020–Present · X32 / Dante · ProPresenter 7 · Fiber

A church planted in 1904, with a tech setup that felt about that old. I rebuilt it: fiber internet, digital audio, ProPresenter running the cues for sound, video, and lights. Sunday stopped being a gamble.

Then the part that never stops — a new series every few weeks, title loops, announcements, and a brand to hold it all together. A small volunteer team runs it. I’m now designing the systems for a new building.
