My Story

As a kid, I was either drawing or building. I loved to see how my ideas would take shape. When drawing characters wasn't enough to fulfill my visions, I made movies to give them life; when drawing houses was too flat, I built with Legos. When Legos looked cool but sat on a shelf, I built furniture. Unknown to me at the time, creativity was the launchpad for the processes and systems to make something useful, things that resonate with people.

As an adult working through free lance. church media, and teaching graphics and video production, I learned that the best results come from a solid framework. Today, I optimize workflows that produce high-quality experiences for the audience by integrating creative strategies, technical infrastructure, and talent development.
